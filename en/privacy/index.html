iMonitor Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

iMonitor does not collect data from the app. It has no analytics or statistics, no third-party SDKs, no account, no advertising, and no tracking across apps or websites.

Who we are

iMonitor is provided by Shenzhen UniversalWind Technology Co., Ltd. In this policy, “iMonitor,” “we,” and “us” refer to the app and its provider. This policy applies to the iMonitor iOS app (com.uwind.imonitor) and this support website.

Data processed on your device

iMonitor reads system and network information made available by iOS to calculate and display information such as storage, memory, overall CPU activity, battery state, uptime, device model, OS version, and network usage.

Your data-plan settings, network-usage history, alert thresholds, preferences, and purchase-entitlement state stay on your device. This local data may be shared between the iMonitor app and its widgets through the app's private App Group container. The app and widgets process it on device and do not send it to us. Local notifications are scheduled and delivered using Apple's on-device notification system.

iMonitor does not provide an account, cloud sync, advertising, analytics, statistics, or tracking. It contains no third-party SDKs. We do not receive, retain, sell, or share your monitoring data.

Purchases and restoration

Apple processes App Store purchases, subscriptions, refunds, and purchase restoration through the system StoreKit service. iMonitor receives only the product and transaction-entitlement information needed on your device to unlock features. The developer does not receive your payment-card or other payment information. Apple's handling of purchases is governed by Apple's own policies.

Device backups

Depending on your iOS and backup settings, locally stored app data may be included in a device backup managed by Apple. iMonitor does not operate a backup server and does not receive that backup.

Support email

If you choose to email support, we receive the email address, message, and attachments you send. This communication happens outside the app and is used only to answer your request, troubleshoot the issue, and maintain necessary support records. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, precise location, or other sensitive information.

We retain support correspondence only as long as reasonably needed to resolve the request, meet legal obligations, and prevent abuse. You may ask us to delete your support correspondence by emailing feather.chan@foxmail.com, subject to any information we must retain by law.

Retention, deletion, and your choices

Because the app does not collect or transmit your monitoring data, we do not retain it on our servers. You can delete history and settings using controls available in the app. Deleting the app removes its locally stored data and App Group data from the device, subject to any device backups controlled by you and Apple. You can change notification permissions in iOS Settings.

This website

This site contains static information and does not use forms, advertising, analytics, or tracking cookies. GitHub Pages may process technical request information, including IP addresses, to deliver and secure the site under the GitHub Privacy Statement. This website-hosting activity is separate from the iMonitor app's App Store privacy disclosure.

Children

iMonitor is not directed to children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect personal data from children or other users.

Changes

We may update this policy if iMonitor's features, providers, or legal obligations change. We will post the revised policy here and update the date above. Material changes will be reflected in the app and App Store disclosures as appropriate.

Contact

For privacy questions, email feather.chan@foxmail.com.
