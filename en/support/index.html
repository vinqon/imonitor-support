iMonitor Support

iMonitor is a local-first iPhone system and data-usage monitor. For help, email feather.chan@foxmail.com.

When reporting a problem, include your iPhone model, iOS version, iMonitor version, and the steps that reproduce it. Screenshots are helpful, but please remove phone numbers, IP addresses, account details, or other sensitive information first.

Frequently asked questions

How accurate are iMonitor’s system readings?

iMonitor shows estimates calculated from information iOS makes available to apps. Values can differ from other tools because sampling times and calculation methods differ. iOS does not allow iMonitor to list processes, show each app’s CPU use, or report battery health.

Why doesn’t data usage match my carrier bill?

iMonitor estimates Wi-Fi and cellular traffic observed by the device. System restarts, counter behavior, delayed sampling, roaming, carrier rounding, billing-cycle rules, and traffic the carrier classifies differently can create a difference. Treat your carrier’s records as authoritative for billing.

Can iMonitor show data usage for each app?

No. iOS does not provide third-party apps with reliable per-app network-usage data. iMonitor does not use a VPN profile to inspect your traffic.

How do I set up my data plan?

Open the Data Usage section, enter your allowance and billing-cycle start day, then enable the alerts you want. iMonitor schedules threshold reminders locally on your device. Notifications require permission in iOS Settings.

Why isn’t a widget updating every second?

iOS controls when home-screen, Lock Screen, and StandBy widgets refresh. A widget may show the most recently available sample rather than a continuously updating value. Open iMonitor for the newest foreground reading.

What is included with Pro?

Pro unlocks the complete widget and theme set, data alerts and multiple plans, longer history and CSV export, and alternate app icons. The exact features shown in the current App Store version and in-app purchase screen control.

I bought the original iMonitor before version 2.0. Do I have to pay again?

No. People who purchased this original iMonitor app before version 2.0 are eligible for lifetime Pro. Update the app and use Restore Purchases if Pro is not unlocked automatically. This promise applies to the original iMonitor App Store record; contact support if your purchase is not recognized.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open iMonitor Settings, select Pro, and choose Restore Purchases while signed in to the Apple Account used for the purchase. If restoration still fails, restart the app and contact support with the product name and approximate purchase date. Do not email receipts containing sensitive billing details unless support specifically requests a redacted copy.

How do I cancel a subscription or request a refund?

Apple handles billing, cancellation, and refunds. Manage a subscription in your Apple Account’s subscription settings. Refund eligibility is decided by Apple through Report a Problem.

Does iMonitor connect to the internet or collect my data?

Your monitoring data, data-plan settings, and usage history stay on your device, including the private App Group container shared with widgets. iMonitor has no analytics or statistics, contains no third-party SDKs, requires no account, has no ads, and does not track you. Apple handles purchases and restoration through the system StoreKit service; the developer does not receive your payment information. See the Privacy Policy for details.
